I build and deploy AI systems that reach real customers. I publish about applied research that ships.

Email · LinkedIn · GitHub

I am an Applied Scientist II at Amazon, where I build and deploy AI systems that reach customers across international marketplaces. My work centers on chatbots — agentic support systems and their evaluation, retrieval-augmented generation, and large language models — and on computer vision for on-device video processing, frame selection, and vision-language models. Earlier, I worked on graph neural networks for large-scale recommendation and abuse detection.

I hold an M.Tech in Computational & Data Science from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), where I worked on domain adaptation and computer vision — active learning and smoothness-based training — at the Vision & AI Lab (VAL).

For details of my work and roles, see Experience and Publications.
