Short Bio

Arihant Jain is an Applied Scientist II at Amazon in Returns & Product Support Science, building AI systems that reach customers across international marketplaces. Since joining Amazon in October 2021, Arihant has worked on graph neural networks for product recommendation and concession-abuse detection, LLM-based troubleshooting chatbots launched across 8 international stores and integrated into Amazon's Rufus assistant, and on-device video frame selection for vision-language models.

Arihant received an M.Tech in Computational and Data Science from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) in 2021, advised by Dr. R. Venkatesh Babu at the Vision & AI Lab, and a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Bharati Vidyapeeth's College of Engineering (GGSIPU), Delhi, in 2019.

Research interests span large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, task-oriented dialogue and its evaluation, vision-language models, graph neural networks, and domain adaptation, with applications in product support and recommender systems.
